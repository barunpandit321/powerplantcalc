About PowerPlantCalc

3. Industrial Standard Compliance (IAPWS-IF97)

All water and steam property calculations on PowerPlantCalc are powered by the IAPWS-IF97 (International Association for the Properties of Water and Steam 1997 Industrial Formulation).

  • Full 5-Region Evaluation: Accurately computes subcooled liquid water (Region 1), superheated steam (Region 2), supercritical fluids (Region 3), two-phase saturation (Region 4), and high-temperature steam up to 2000°C (Region 5).
  • 16 Thermodynamic Metrics: Evaluates enthalpy, entropy, specific volume, density, internal energy, heat capacities (Cp, Cv), speed of sound, dynamic viscosity, and thermal conductivity.
  • Interactive Mollier (h-s) & T-s Diagrams: Features dynamic HTML5 Canvas thermodynamic state diagrams with operating point markers.
