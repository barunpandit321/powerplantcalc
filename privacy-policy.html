Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026 — PowerPlantCalc.com Transparency Commitment

At PowerPlantCalc.com ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our website handles information when you use our online power plant engineering tools, calculators, and technical documentation.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PowerPlantCalc.com is designed as a free, open-access engineering suite. We do not require user account registration, user logins, passwords, names, physical addresses, or credit card information. You can freely perform thermodynamic calculations without disclosing any personal identity.

2. Browser Local Storage & Technical Preferences

To enhance your user experience, our calculators use your browser's native LocalStorage to remember your preferred settings:

  • Theme Choice: Remembers whether you prefer Light Mode or Dark Mode.
  • Unit Preferences: Remembers your chosen input and output units (e.g., kg/cm² (g) vs bar (abs), °C vs °F, kcal/kg vs kJ/kg).
  • Input Values: Saves your last entered calculation parameters so you don't lose progress upon reloading.

This data is stored 100% locally inside your web browser on your device and is never transmitted or sold to external servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cookies and site data.

3. Server Logs & Analytics

Like most standard web servers, our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages) automatically logs basic non-personally-identifiable request data for security, diagnostic, and performance monitoring purposes. This may include your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, operating language, referring page, and request timestamps.

4. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We do not use invasive tracking cookies. We may use standard web performance optimization services (such as Cloudflare) to ensure fast CDN delivery, DDoS protection, and SSL encryption. If third-party advertising or analytics services (such as Google AdSense or Google Analytics) are integrated in the future, they may use cookies in compliance with standard Google Privacy Guidelines and GDPR/CCPA regulations.

5. Shareable Deep-Links & URL Parameters

Our Steam Calculator features a 1-click "Share Link" generator that embeds calculation mode and unit choices directly into the page URL query parameters (e.g., ?mode=PT&v1=70&v2=490). These parameters are handled client-side in your browser to reload the specific calculation state and contain zero private user information.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website provides technical power plant calculation tools intended for engineers, plant operators, students, and researchers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect site improvements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
